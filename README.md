# The Scramble

A strategy game about advising the President through the moment the US decides to pace frontier AI. Built from "The Scramble: getting in position to pace the frontier" and the AIPI pacing concept note (v3). People and companies are fictional; places, mechanisms and numbers follow the note.

Open `index.html` in a browser. The game saves to localStorage after every move.

## Files

- `engine.js`: the whole simulation (state, actions, events, China, labs, endings). No DOM; runs in node.
- `ui.js`, `index.html`: rendering only.
- `sim/playtest.js [games] [difficulty] [bot]`: plays scripted strategies (random, expert, hawk, hawksec, hawkpure, passive, early, noprep, reader, noauto, dpa, nosec, sl4only) and prints the endings.
- `sim/trace.js [seed] [bot]`: one game, turn by turn.
- `sim/loc.js [bot]`: breaks down what the bot's losses came from.
- `sim/levers.js`: months for China to catch a paced frontier under each lever.
- `sim/strategy.js`: a strategy with 132 adjustable choices; its default settings reproduce the scripted expert.
- `sim/hillclimb.js [gens] [games] [difficulty] [run]`: searches for better settings on 12 CPU cores and checks the winner on 4,000 fresh games. Compare rule changes using the average of 3 runs; single runs vary by about ±1.5 points. `PIN='{"seq":"together"}'` holds genes fixed, to find the best play down one path.
- `sim/narrate.js [genome.json]`: writes `sim/playbook.md`, which describes a strategy in plain English, says what each choice is worth, and walks through one game turn by turn.
- `sim/mechs.js [genome.json]`: win rate with each pacing mechanism forced, split by the game's court climate, and what choosing per game is worth.
- `sim/ablate.js`, `sim/uae.js`, `sim/hostile.js`: one-off analyses (what each choice is worth, the UAE choice, credibility lost to hostile principals).

Tunables for the UAE event and the President's hawk bias (`hawkBase`, `hawkProvoke`, `hawkRace`) live in `TUNE` in `engine.js`.

`index.html#autotest` plays random games through the real UI and sets `body[data-test]` to PASS or FAIL. `#demo-p0`, `#demo-scramble`, `#demo-p1` and `#demo-end` fast-forward a seeded game for screenshots.

## Model in one paragraph

Capability is in months of US frontier progress. Unpaced labs gain 1.0/month, minus "drag" from our own moves (SL4 5%, or SL5 30% easing to 15% over 12 months, instead; control protocols 5%, chip and cloud rules 1.5–3%, mineral curbs and Strait disruption while they last). Drag also lowers the markets' resting level and the labs' goodwill. Paced labs gain 0.12/month with automation limits and 0.40 without. China gains `0.16 + 0.25 × compute + 0.34 × distillation + 0.22 × methods leak` (the copying terms ×0.4 when the US is paced). This follows metr_graph's split of China's pace (own research 0.26, compute 0.15, distillation 0.27, leaks 0.22, in US-pace units), with compute raised to 0.25 as a hedge since the split measures compute growth, not a sudden cut. Its own-research term (0.16) is a hidden per-game lognormal (80% range 0.07–0.35). While the US is paced, distillation falls with the gap left over the gap at the pause, and leaked methods fade over 12 months, as in metr_graph. Stopping the leak cuts 90% of it, absorbed over about a year. China starts a hidden lognormal 6 months behind (80% range 4.1–8.8). Baseline compute follows IFP's end-2026 channel stocks: domestic 34% (HBM and tool curbs can cut it by up to a twentieth within the game), offshore 20%, smuggled 20%, legal 16% and 10% other. The distillation crackdown cuts harvested outputs ~95%, which at ~25% of the gain per 10× of data leaves China 68% of its distillation boost. Each game hides its own offshore (0.5–1.5× baseline), smuggling (0.4–2.4×, log-uniform) and distillation (60–100%) levels, and a channel no rule covers can grow unannounced (4% a month each). Intelligence quality also scales how far the indicator markers and the IC's standing bias sit from the true threshold (1.2 - 0.8 × quality/100). Phase 1 cheating alerts carry an IC confidence word blurred by weak intelligence, and weak intelligence adds false alarms (`TUNE.falseAlarm` × (1 - quality/100) a month). At the Phase 2 summit, Beijing hid a program with chance 2.5 × (0.85 - enforcement), where enforcement is half intelligence and half survey and inspections (certain if it is cheating undetected): the `betrayed` ending. Each game also hides how hostile the courts are (0.5–1.5×; counsel gives a word, a number after the legal study), Beijing's cyber push on US labs (0.5–1.8× theft attempts) and its concealment (0.5–1.5× on cheating and hidden-program risk; GEOINT cuts 60% of the departure from 1), and draws Beijing's starting factions and each lab's starting goodwill. The pause starts on executive authority with a monthly injunction chance (`TUNE.courtRate`: DPA 30%, licensing 20%, Charter 10%, DPA once any lab is compelled; × court climate, ×0.4 with the legal study); a statute cuts it to 4% (`TUNE.statuteCourt`), and without one a court voids Phase 2 after signing 35% of the time. A statute needs 60 Senate votes to send and 55 on the floor two months later, where the count lands ±9 (`TUNE.whip`, one s.d.) off the whip count; a failed bill waits 8 months. Senate votes start from a base of 0 (`TUNE.senateBase`), and Hill work tops out at +20 (`TUNE.hillCap`). Labs bound without signing voluntarily drain Senate votes each month (`TUNE.labLobby` × (0.25 + ruthlessness)), all labs ×8 while a bill is moving (voluntary signers at half); below 52 votes a rider can cut verification funding. Without a statute a Charter signatory can walk out (`TUNE.walkRate`), likelier near China or with shaky compliance. A meeting with the same principal within two turns of the last does 60% as much (`TUNE.meetDecay`), and each favor costs 2 credibility per other favor in the last four turns. The Beijing tab shows these as words, ranges or numbers depending on intelligence quality (under 45, 45–69, 70+; an IC assessment counts +20). Weight theft: Beijing attempts in 5% of months (`TUNE.theftBase`) ×(0.5 + 1.5 × (frontier / RSI threshold)²), capped at ×1 under a deal, (×2.2 racing, ×1.3 when the US is paced, ×1.3 when more than 6 months behind); an attempt succeeds 90% at SL3, 50% at SL4 and 4% at SL5. SL4 takes the executive order plus 2 actions implementing it; SL5 takes 6 more actions after that and costs labs -20, industry -12 and markets -6. A hidden RSI threshold (lognormal, median 12.5 months out on Advisor) sets the runaway hazard per month for any actor past it: 30% for an uncovered lab, 2% for a covered lab with automation limits, and 15% for China without a deal.
