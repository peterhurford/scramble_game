# The Scramble

A strategy game about advising the President through the moment the US decides to pace frontier AI. Built from "The Scramble: getting in position to pace the frontier" and the AIPI pacing concept note (v3). People and companies are fictional; places, mechanisms and numbers follow the note.

Open `index.html` in a browser. The game saves to localStorage after every move.

## Files

- `engine.js`: the whole simulation (state, actions, events, China, labs, endings). No DOM; runs in node.
- `ui.js`, `index.html`: rendering only.
- `room/`: the Room Edition, a seven-turn, 30-minute version for a room split into three tables (White House, State, Pentagon), each picking one of three standing moves a turn (a crisis adds a fourth to one table), plus one bold move for the room (open `room/index.html`; rules in `room/engine.js`). `node room/sim.js` prints how scripted rooms end.
- `sim/playtest.js [games] [difficulty] [bot]`: plays scripted strategies (random, expert, hawk, hawksec, hawkpure, passive, early, noprep, reader, noauto, dpa, nosec, sl4only) and prints the endings.
- `sim/trace.js [seed] [bot]`: one game, turn by turn.
- `sim/loc.js [bot]`: breaks down what the bot's losses came from.
- `sim/levers.js [month]`: months for China to catch a paused frontier under each lever, with the alone floor off (`0.4` pauses at the start; default Jul 2027).
- `sim/strategy.js`: a strategy with 132 adjustable choices; its default settings reproduce the scripted expert.
- `sim/hillclimb.js [gens] [games] [difficulty] [run]`: searches for better settings on 12 worker threads and checks the winner on 4,000 fresh games. Compare rule changes using the average of 3 runs; single runs vary by about ±1.5 points. `RUNS=0,1,2` runs all three in one process (so they all use the code loaded at the start), `OUT=sim/v13` writes `sim/v13_run<N>.json` instead of overwriting `best_genome.json`, `PIN='{"seq":"together"}'` holds genes fixed, and `sim/.search.lock` makes a second search wait for the first. Children race: all play the first third of the batch, and only those within 0.03 of the parent (`RACE_MARGIN`) play the rest.
- `sim/narrate.js [genome.json]`: writes `sim/playbook.md`, which describes a strategy in plain English, says what each choice is worth, and walks through one game turn by turn.
- `sim/mechs.js [genome.json]`: win rate with each pacing mechanism forced, split by the game's court climate, and what choosing per game is worth.
- `sim/doom.js [games]`: fits the IC's p(doom) (`DOOM_W` in engine.js) on bot games and prints its held-out calibration.
- `sim/ablate.js`, `sim/uae.js`, `sim/hostile.js`: one-off analyses (what each choice is worth, the UAE choice, credibility lost to hostile principals).

Luck is narrowed by four `TUNE` knobs (`spread 1, spreadRead 1, luckFix 0, fuse 1` plays the old dice): `spread` (0) takes out the IC's blind miscalibration, so the indicators sit on the true threshold; `spreadT` (0.7) keeps most of the RSI threshold's range, so reading the indicators pays; while `spreadRead` (1) leaves the draws the player can read or shape (courts, China's starting point and channels, cyber push, concealment, factions, lab goodwill) at full range; `luckFix` (0.5) evens out streaks in the President's, Beijing's and the labs' answers while keeping each long-run rate at the odds shown; catastrophes (runaways, China's win, thefts, Strait crises, Beijing starting to cheat, injunctions, walkouts) fire when their summed hazard crosses a hidden gamma fuse of shape `fuse` (4) and mean `fuseMean` (0.65), so low risk reliably pays and piled-up risk reliably doesn't.

Each pacing mechanism hangs on a condition the player can read: with `situ` (1), a DPA halt on the courts, the Charter's walkouts on lab goodwill (rate × e^(-(goodwill - `walkMid`) / `walkScale`), -40 and 20), licensing's court risk and leaks on Commerce; with `dpaStick` (1), an injunction against DPA orders without a statute is final (no appeal, defying it loses coverage, no compelling until Congress acts). `sim/strategy.js` gene `labMeet` meets the coolest lab CEO every N turns in Phase 0.

Labs have agendas with `labAI` (1; 0 plays the old dice-only labs exactly, checked by `compare.js --tune '{"labAI":0}'` against the previous commit). `labFear` (safety culture, nearness of the lab's own models to where the lab believes the threshold is, scares; the belief is off by a fixed per-game log sd `labBlind` 0.2, so the worry shown in the Labs panel reads the threshold no better than the IC midpoint) and goodwill (1 lean per `labGood` 40 goodwill; positive goodwill fades `labFade` 8% a month, and repeat CEO meetings within 6 months give `labMeetFade` 0.6x each) set `labPro`, its lean on pacing, which is `labConv` (its conviction about pacing itself) plus goodwill; a lab whose conviction favours pacing but whose goodwill drags its lean below -0.2 backs pacing and swipes at the administration instead (credibility or IC quality -`labSpurn`); `labTurn` gives each lab one diceless move a month (sprint, counter-lobby, court a principal; or warn, brief, back the bill, hold its own run) and queues the offers `lab_preempt`, `lab_pilot`, `lab_pact`. `charterDecide` takes a list of term ids (`LABS[].terms`; genes `term.<id>`), a walkout is preceded by `ultimatum` with `labNotice` months to answer the named complaint or raise its goodwill by `labStay` (20; a granted term gives 12, so a term alone does not hold it), and a compliant signatory raises detection of its rival by `labWatch`.

The side panel shows the IC's p(doom): it opens at `TUNE.doomOpen` (6%) and moves toward the fitted chance of a bad ending as the IC wakes up (`doomWake`: months elapsed, the IC's threshold estimate, alarming events, leaving Phase 0), blurred by a slow random walk that weak intelligence widens. With chance `TUNE.fun` (0.35) a turn, one event from the AI safety movement (`m_*`) or the wider world (`w_*`) fires, apart from the regular roll and drawn from `hash01`, so the main random stream is untouched; `fun: 0` turns them off.

Tunables for the UAE event, the President's hawk bias (`hawkBase`, `hawkProvoke`, `hawkRace`) and the Strait (`strait`, `straitBase`, `straitHot`, `straitHotFrom`, `straitPre`, `warEsc`, `holdFirm`), and how much harder Beijing tries to steal weights while cheating on a deal (`cheatSteal`), live in `TUNE` in `engine.js`.

`index.html#autotest` plays random games through the real UI and sets `body[data-test]` to PASS or FAIL. `#demo-p0`, `#demo-scramble`, `#demo-p1`, `#demo-end`, `#demo-strait`, `#demo-blockade` and `#demo-war` fast-forward a seeded game for screenshots.

## Model in one paragraph

Capability is in months of US frontier progress. Unpaced labs gain 1.0/month, minus "drag" from our own moves (SL4 5%, or SL5 30% easing to 15% over 12 months, instead; control protocols 5%, chip and cloud rules 1.5–3%, mineral curbs and Strait disruption while they last). Drag also lowers the markets' resting level and the labs' goodwill. Paced labs gain 0.12/month with automation limits and 0.40 without. China gains `0.16 + 0.25 × compute + 0.34 × distillation + 0.22 × methods leak` (the copying terms ×0.4 when the US is paced). This follows metr_graph's split of China's pace (own research 0.26, compute 0.15, distillation 0.27, leaks 0.22, in US-pace units), with compute raised to 0.25 as a hedge since the split measures compute growth, not a sudden cut. Its own-research term (0.16) is a hidden per-game lognormal (80% range 0.07–0.35). While the US is paced, distillation falls with the gap left over the gap at the pause, and leaked methods fade over 12 months, as in metr_graph. Stopping the leak cuts 90% of it, absorbed over about a year. China starts a hidden lognormal 6 months behind (80% range 4.1–8.8). Baseline compute follows IFP's end-2026 channel stocks: domestic 34% (HBM and tool curbs can cut it by up to a twentieth within the game), offshore 20%, smuggled 20%, legal 16% and 10% other. The distillation crackdown cuts harvested outputs ~95%, which at ~25% of the gain per 10× of data leaves China 68% of its distillation boost. Each game hides its own offshore (0.5–1.5× baseline), smuggling (0.4–2.4×, log-uniform) and distillation (60–100%) levels, and a channel no rule covers can grow unannounced (4% a month each). Intelligence quality also scales how far the indicator markers and the IC's standing bias sit from the true threshold (1.2 - 0.8 × quality/100). Phase 1 cheating alerts carry an IC confidence word blurred by weak intelligence, and weak intelligence adds false alarms (`TUNE.falseAlarm` × (1 - quality/100) a month). At the Phase 2 summit, Beijing hid a program with chance 2.5 × (0.85 - enforcement), where enforcement is half intelligence and half survey and inspections (certain if it is cheating undetected): the `betrayed` ending. Each game also hides how hostile the courts are (0.5–1.5×; counsel gives a word, a number after the legal study), Beijing's cyber push on US labs (0.4–2.5× theft attempts, log-uniform) and its concealment (0.5–1.5× on cheating and hidden-program risk; GEOINT cuts 60% of the departure from 1), and draws Beijing's starting factions and each lab's starting goodwill. The pause starts on executive authority with a monthly injunction chance (`TUNE.courtRate`: DPA 30%, licensing 20%, Charter 10%, DPA once any lab is compelled; × court climate, ×0.4 with the legal study); a statute cuts it to 4% (`TUNE.statuteCourt`), and without one a court voids Phase 2 after signing 35% of the time. A statute needs 60 Senate votes to send and 55 on the floor two months later, where the count lands ±9 (`TUNE.whip`, one s.d.) off the whip count; a failed bill waits 8 months. Senate votes start from a base of 0 (`TUNE.senateBase`), and Hill work tops out at +25 (`TUNE.hillCap`). At the midterms the opposition takes the Senate 56-44 and the House, as on 3 Nov 2026 (`TUNE.flipAlways`; at 0, chance logistic((3.4 - 0.4 × (markets - 100) - 0.1 × salience) / 3), 55% at the start). The opposition Senate adds 7 votes, one a seat of swing (`TUNE.senateFlip`; 3 when the flip was 52-48) and each Work the Hill gives 0.7× as much (`TUNE.flipHill`); its investigations, in the Senate or the House, arrive as a random event (40% a turn, 30% without the House, at most every 4 turns) that raises salience +3 or +6, and the opposition House halves the rider's chance (`TUNE.houseFlip`). If the President's party holds the Senate, you can instead ask a friendly chair for an investigation (1 capital and no action, every 3 turns at most): salience +8, industry -6. Labs bound without signing voluntarily drain Senate votes each month (`TUNE.labLobby` × (0.25 + ruthlessness)), all labs ×3 while a bill is moving (voluntary signers at half); below 52 votes a rider can cut verification funding. Without a statute a Charter signatory can walk out (`TUNE.walkRate`), likelier near China or with shaky compliance. US politics (pacing support, the Senate, the President, the labs) read the IC's estimate of the gap (`seenLead`); Beijing acts on the true gap. Senate votes gain 0.6 per month of perceived lead above 2 (from -1.8 to +4.8); support loses 3.5 per month the perceived lead falls short of 5 (at most 18); support under 12 (`TUNE.supportBreak`) breaks the pause at once. Beijing's appetite for a deal gains `TUNE.leadSlope` (0.2) per month of true lead above 4 (from -1.6 to +1.6) and loses up to `TUNE.overtakePen` (2.0) when at today's rates China would pass the US frontier within 6 months. Past the RSI threshold without a binding deal, China has a `TUNE.cnWin` (10%) × nearness monthly chance of getting RSI working and keeping control: the `cnwins` ending. As a game-balance choice rather than an estimate, each channel a control has cut also slows China's own research by up to `TUNE.ctrlBite` (legal chips 20%, offshore 12%, smuggling 10%, distillation 8%). The President's resistance to hawk moves is `TUNE.hawkBase` = -0.075. The US can cheat too: a secret training run (a covert action during the pause) keeps the leading lab advancing 0.12 + `TUNE.secretRate` (0.5) a month at an unpaced lab's risk; each month Beijing catches it with chance `TUNE.usCatch` (0.1) + 0.25 if the deal has inspections + 0.1 × (cyber push - 1), and Phase 2 inspectors find a running one 70% of the time. Under a deal Beijing also catches a cheating US lab with chance `TUNE.cnSeesUS` (0.15) a month. Catching America cheating, or a denial, leaves Beijing 1.6× likelier to cheat for six months. Phase 0 gets one Oval decision a month (two a turn). Under a deal, Beijing's chance of starting to cheat is scaled by (1.4 - 0.8 × trust/100) × (1 + 0.15 per control still in force) (`TUNE.cheatTrust`, `TUNE.cheatResent`). Beijing accepts an offer with probability logistic(`TUNE.cnSharp` (3) × utility); its posture each month carries noise of `TUNE.postureNoise` (2). Export controls are leverage: an offer to resume chip sales is worth `TUNE.chipLever` (0.55) more to Beijing while the chip ban stands, and Entity List relief `TUNE.reliefLever` (0.25) more for each other control in place (remote access, distillation, smuggling); the "every channel closed" penalty doesn't apply when the offer reopens one. Track 2 gives Beijing trust +12 and the science faction +5. Each mechanism has its own condition: DPA injunctions suspend every lab the orders hold, and appeals win 0.5 - 0.5 × (court climate - 1) of the time (`TUNE.appealSwing`); a voluntary Charter signer's compliance gains 0.3 × its goodwill (`TUNE.charterRel`); licensing compliance gains 0.3 × the Commerce Secretary's relationship (`TUNE.licRel`), Commerce exemptions come only from a Commerce that isn't Friendly, and an Ally's lawyers halve its court risk. Offering the joint institute costs the Secretary of War 4, the DNI 3 and 3 Senate votes for 6 months (`TUNE.inst`); a deal that includes it adds Beijing trust +8, the science faction +3 and inspections +2. A meeting with the same principal within two months of the last does 60% as much (`TUNE.meetDecay`), and each favor costs 2 credibility per other favor in the last four months. A turn covers two months in Phase 0 (`TUNE.slowMonths`, with `TUNE.p0Ap` = 1 more action a turn), one otherwise (a Strait crisis, the knock or a runaway ends a long turn early), with one random event a turn (`TUNE.eventsPerTurn`). Every timer counts months (two days a tick in the scramble), not turns. The Beijing tab shows these as words, ranges or numbers depending on intelligence quality (under 45, 45–69, 70+; an IC assessment counts +20). Weight theft: Beijing attempts in 5% of months (`TUNE.theftBase`) ×(0.5 + 1.5 × (frontier / RSI threshold)²), capped at ×1 under a deal, (×2.2 racing, ×1.3 when the US is paced, ×1.3 when more than 6 months behind); an attempt succeeds 90% at SL3, 25% at SL4 and 4% at SL5. SL4 takes the executive order plus 1 action implementing it; SL5 takes 3 more actions after that and costs labs -20, industry -12 and markets -6. A hidden RSI threshold (lognormal, median 12 months out on Advisor (13.4 Analyst, 11.3 ExComm)) sets the runaway hazard per month for any actor past it: 30% for an uncovered lab, 2% for a covered lab with automation limits, and 15% for China without a deal.

The Strait: each month without a crisis Beijing starts one with chance (`TUNE.straitBase` 1% + 0.12 × ((temperature − 25)/40)² above 25 (12% at 65) + 1.2% racing + up to 17% for a US lead over 3 months near the threshold, ×0.35 paced) × (0.6 + Security/75) × (0.3 with a deal). Most crises start as a quarantine of Taiwan; 30–90% go straight to war, more when hot and when the motive is preventive. A quarantine slows US labs 18%, cuts China's smuggled, legal and half its offshore chips by 40%, and turns into war 18–30% a month (×1.8 with convoys, ×0.6 while talking). War is a phase: US labs 30% slower (drag cap 60%), China keeps 20% of imported chips, markets -20 then -2 a month, deals and the summit are off, theft ×1.8, pacing support -15 and Senate -10. Escalation climbs about 3.5 a month (×`TUNE.warEsc`) with a 15% monthly jump of 20; at 100 the game ends (`war`). A ceasefire comes from the player's offer, Beijing's terms, or exhaustion (4% + 2.5% per month). A kinetic strike always starts a war. Still at war in December 2028 is the `wartime` ending. The IC reads the risk as a word, range or number, by intelligence quality.

Beijing's moves: every `TUNE.cnPlanGap` (3) months outside a war, quarantine or the scramble, Beijing plays its best-scoring move (`cnMoves`; `TUNE.cnPlan` 0 turns all three off). It answers each export control once (`cnRetaliate`): mineral curbs while US labs race, a squeeze on US firms in China once they are paced. It courts the ally the coalition leans on (`cnWedge`, coalition 35+, likelier with more controls, a standing threat or a coalition near the 50-62 margin). Within 4 turns of a rejected offer it returns with its own package (`cnCounter`: the freeze, threats and, with Security above 32, inspections out; its keystone and up to two carrots in). It also rebuilds `TUNE.cnReroute` (30%) of the compute a control cuts within about a year, through offshore or smuggling if no rule covers them, else domestic fabs at half speed. `TUNE.cnWait` (0.5) makes it slower to sign while the US pause looks likely to fall on its own (thin support, an injunction, a deadline within 3 months, coverage under 70%), and a statute earns 0.3 × that back. `TUNE.cnAim` moves its posture toward racing when a paced US with no deal can be caught within a year and toward engaging when it is far behind or cheating under a deal; `TUNE.cnCheatAim` scales the chance it starts cheating from ×1.5 at a US lead of 2 months or less to ×0.5 at 8 or more. Beijing also counts what the US could still do to it (`usLeverage`, 0-1: 0.4 per control in force, 0.4 for a standing threat, up to 0.5 for a lead past 4 months). With little of it (`TUNE.cnExploit`), it pockets a unilateral pause (up to -0.4 on signing) and is up to ×1.6 likelier to start cheating under a deal; and under a deal it demands one more carrot, twice a deal at most (`cnPress`). The player answers these moves through the cabinet: Commerce compensates squeezed firms (1 capital if its Secretary is at 0 or better, else 2 and a worse result), Treasury signs the carve-out that outbids a wedge (Treasury -8; coalition +2 at 0 or better, else -6), State carries a refusal (clean at 15 or better) and the President signs any added term. State carries China diplomacy (`TUNE.cnState`): the Secretary of State's relationship over 40, capped at ±1, adds ±0.25 to Beijing's view of every offer (so the Phase 2 summit too), ±0.1 to a threat's credibility and to reassurance, and ±0.06 to a ceasefire (at 0, only an Ally or Hostile Secretary moves offers). A Secretary of State below -5 sits on an offer, threat, sounding, guarantee or ceasefire with chance `TUNE.cnVeto` × (-relationship - 5) / 80, up to 40%: the action is spent, the capital is not; each of those actions shows the Secretary's tier and the veto odds. Two coercion ladders are moves too, each taken in a given month only with chance score / 2. The economic war (`cnEcon`) climbs from a mineral embargo to an entity list on US champions to a run on Treasuries (markets -5, -8, -12 at once, then -0.6 a month per rung until it ends); controls, a grievance, a racing US lead, a weak market and the rungs already climbed push it up, Beijing's Market faction pulls it down and may end it, and a deal ends it. Treasury can steady markets, Commerce can hit back (a rung down on a roll) and State can seek a truce (it ends on a roll). The Strait ladder (`cnStrait`: drills, then customs inspections at sea) answers a big US lead near the threshold; each rung adds 0.012 to the monthly crisis odds until a carrier group or State talks it down (a roll each) or Beijing steps down once the US pauses, deals or loses the lead. Theft is planned (`cnTheft`): when the lead is worth stealing and lab security weak, Beijing opens a 3-month operation with attempts ×2.5; the IC spots it with chance 0.2 + 0.6 × intelligence quality, and then the lab can be locked down (success ×0.4) or State can warn Beijing off (a roll). Beijing also fears what is being built (`cnAlarm`, 0-1): as either side nears the threshold and after warning shots, near-misses and bio scares, its factions shift toward Science and a deal is worth up to +0.3 more to it. All of these at 0 is the Beijing that only drifted. The Beijing tab lists what it is doing under "Beijing's play".

Misuse: what criminals and rogue agents can reach is the best open model (Beijing's frontier until open weights are modeled) or the US frontier two months back through jailbroken APIs, plus scaffolding that improves 0.15 a month even while training is paced, over the RSI threshold. Monthly AI-enabled cyberattack odds are (4% + 30% × level²) (×0.45 with the defenders-first project, ×1.5 at war); bio near-misses start at level 0.45 and reach 16% a month at 1 (×0.6 with synthesis screening, which also turns the event into a credibility gain). Sovereign agents (0-100) grow 6 × (level - 0.3) a month and decay 3%, ×0.3 under compute KYC; they cost markets agents/80 a month and, while paced above 20, up to 8 points of pacing support. Sovereign agents run on open weights, so they scale the open-weight runaway hazard: `TUNE.openHaz` × (1 + `TUNE.agentOpen` (1.5) × (agents − `TUNE.agentBase` (33))/100), floored at half; 33 is the hazard-weighted agent level under the v27 genome, so openHaz keeps its meaning. A deal's joint misuse term (worth 0.02 + 0.25 × level to Beijing, more with its science faction, less with Security; costs DNI `TUNE.pactDni` (8) and State `TUNE.pactSec` (6) to offer) halves bio near-misses, multiplies agents' growth by `TUNE.pactAgent` (0.7), speeds their decay, and cuts cyberattacks 20%. After a near-miss with no screening and level above 0.8, an outbreak comes at 4% a month (markets -15, credibility -8; the scramble in Phase 0, pacing support +15 for six months in Phase 1). Each month at most one principal acts on their own: an Ally or Friendly one helps, a Cool or Hostile one hurts (8% × influence/60 for Ally or Hostile, 3% for Friendly or Cool; × `TUNE.cabOdds` (3) for a helpful move, × `TUNE.cabHurt` (1) for a harmful one). Every relationship drifts 4% a month toward 0 (`TUNE.relDrift`). Each principal also has a standing effect at Ally (40+) and its mirror at Hostile (-40 or less), listed in `TIER_FX`: VP pacing support ±6, Chief of Staff ±1 Oval decision, Treasury markets ±0.6 a month, Commerce licensing and smuggling, State ±0.25 on Beijing's acceptance, War Beijing temperature ∓0.5 a month, DNI intelligence quality ±1 a month. An Ally takes half of any loss (`TUNE.allySticky`), a meeting gives `TUNE.meetBase` (16), an ask ignored for `TUNE.askNeglect` (6) months costs 3, and Commerce takes `TUNE.docIndustry` (0.25) of every industry hit. `TUNE.misuse` scales the incident odds and `TUNE.agents` the agents' growth. Events show a different variant each time they repeat (`nth`), and a principal's asks skip any whose moment has passed.
